# EXPLORE SECTION

1. ### Search
    search bar (recent searcher & trending search)

2. ### category
    Visual icons + names in a 2-column grid (not just the pill row from Home).
    Tapping a category filters everything below.
    Keep it compact — 6 to 8 core categories max.

3. ###  Curated Collections
    Group courses into themes: "Get Hired as a Developer", "Design Fundamentals", "Free This Week".
    These feel editorial, not algorithmic — more trustworthy.
    One horizontal strip per collection.

4. ### Filter Bar

    Horizontal chips: Difficulty (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced), Duration (< 5 hrs, 5–20 hrs, 20+ hrs), Price (Free, Paid, On Sale), Rating.
    Guests use this more than logged-in users.

5. ### "New & Noteworthy" Strip

    Courses launched in the last 30 days.
    Gives returning users a reason to check Explore again.

6. ### Instructor Spotlight

    Horizontal cards showing top instructors with their course count + avg rating.
    People follow people, not just topics.

7. ### Career / Skill Paths (if you have them)

    Multi-course sequences: "Become a Frontend Dev" → 5 courses in order.
    Big differentiator from just listing individual courses.

8. ### "Because You Viewed…" (logged-in only)

    If they tapped a course but didn't enroll, show similar ones here.
    Keep it subtle — one strip, not the whole screen.
